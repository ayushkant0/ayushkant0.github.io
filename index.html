01 / About

From AI and data science to securing mobile networks

I completed my B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science at the Indian Institute of Information Technology, Design and Manufacturing (IIITDM) Kurnool, with a minor in Unmanned Aerial Vehicles.

Today I'm a researcher at IIT Bombay in the Department of Electrical Engineering, working on 5G security. My work brings machine learning to the 5G core: deploying open-source cores end to end, studying how their network functions behave, and building systems that spot threats in traffic that is almost all encrypted.

Before that, internships at C-DAC Noida, NITI Aayog and Infosys Springboard took me through system forensics, public-sector work and applied computer vision.

  • 5G core securityStandalone core testbeds, network-function behaviour, attack surface
  • Encrypted traffic analysisIdentifying apps and threats without decrypting payloads
  • ML for networksClassification and anomaly detection on flow metadata
  • Applied securitySteganography, host forensics, data protection

Private 5G security platform2026

KAVACH

Security that sits inside a private 5G network. KAVACH recognises encrypted applications without decrypting them, ties every flow to the subscriber behind it in real time, and maps alerts to MITRE ATT&CK. Operators can stop a single device in one click. Built by Team DRISTI for the TCOE India Emerging Technologies Hackathon 2026.
